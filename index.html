THE ORIGIN STORY

Born from nothing.
Built by everyone.

SHIB was created in August 2020 by an anonymous founder known as Ryoshi. It was an experiment: could a token with zero pre-mine, zero team allocation, and zero central control build something real?

The answer was yes. 50% of supply was deposited into Uniswap and the keys were burned. The other 50% was sent to Vitalik Buterin, who later destroyed over 410 trillion SHIB, one of the largest burns in crypto history.

What started as an experiment has evolved into a thriving ecosystem. The ShibArmy spans over 150 countries, with 28 international Telegram groups in every major language. From DeFi integrations to real-world merchant adoption, the community continues to push boundaries and prove that decentralized, grassroots movements can build something lasting.
